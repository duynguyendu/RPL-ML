(PHY) and media access control (MAC) for Low Rate
Wireless Personal Area Networks (LR-WPANs) \cite{salmanOverviewIEEE8021542010},
targetting devices bounded by low computing
power, memory and energy. It is designed to operate in Personal
Operating Space (POS) of 10 meters or less on three license free

\section{\ieeemacE{} (TSCH)}
The RFC 7554 ammended \ieeemacE{} with the addition of Time-Slotted
Channel Hopping (TSCH). The idea of
TSCH is that nodes would transmit and receive during a time slot,
which would eliminate the need to listen the whole time. Instead, a
node would wake up and listen for first potion of the time slot and
then go to sleep if there is no packets.
Each send is hop to the next channel to avoid collision, the chance is ...

The time is divided into slots, and frequency channels. Each next
transmission would hop to different channels to avoid consuming the
whole channel and collision.
Compare to CSMA/CA, the radio receiver does not have to turn on the
whole period, thus saving energy but potentially delay the message
transmission to the slotted frame.

\section{IPv6 over Low-Power Wireless Personal Area Networks (6LoWPAN)}

dedicated to applying IPv6 to low-power devices operating on
